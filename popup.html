Organize by category (metadata)

Reads Open Graph, JSON-LD, and hostname hints; groups by category. Large categories are split across windows (see per-window cap in limits.js). Large applies are split into automatic batches (see limits.js). Pinned and chrome:// tabs are skipped. On first preview, Chrome may ask for optional site access so Tabby can read metadata on your tabs; if you decline, classification uses URL signals only.
